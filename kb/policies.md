# ServiceMate Policies

## Working hours and areas
We work Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM. We are closed on Sundays.
We serve Jaipur city. Areas outside Jaipur are not covered at the moment.

## Cancellation and rescheduling
Free cancellation or rescheduling up to 2 hours before the booked slot.
Cancellation later than that has a Rs 100 fee.

## Warranty
Repair work carries a 30-day service warranty on the work done. Spare parts
carry the manufacturer warranty. Warranty does not cover damage caused by
misuse or by another technician.

## Safety and verification
All technicians are police-verified and carry an ID card. Customers should
ask to see the ID before the work starts.

## What we cannot do
We do not give medical, legal or financial advice. We do not work on gas
pipelines or on high-voltage industrial equipment.
