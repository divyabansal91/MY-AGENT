# ServiceMate FAQ

## How do I book a service?
Tell the assistant which service you need, the date and a time slot. It will
check availability and ask for your name and phone number to confirm the booking.

## How will I know my booking is confirmed?
You get a booking ID right after booking. Our team may call to confirm the visit.

## Can I change the time after booking?
Yes. Reschedule free of charge up to 2 hours before the slot. Contact the team
with your booking ID.

## What if the technician cannot fix the problem?
Only the visit charge applies. No repair charge is taken if the problem is
not fixed.

## Do you give invoices?
Yes, a digital invoice is shared after the job is completed.
