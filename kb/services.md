# ServiceMate Home Services: Services Offered

(Sample business data for demo purposes. Replace with your own.)

## AC Repair and Service
We repair and service split and window ACs. Includes gas top-up check, filter
cleaning, cooling diagnosis and minor part replacement. Spare parts are charged
extra after the customer approves the quote. Standard service takes about 60 minutes.

## Plumbing
Leakage repair, tap and pipe fitting, flush tank repair, drain unblocking and
water tank connections. Materials are charged extra at actual cost.
Visit takes about 45 minutes.

## Electrician
Fan, switch, socket and light fitting, MCB and wiring fault repair, inverter
connection. Visit takes about 45 minutes. Parts are charged extra.

## Deep Home Cleaning
Full home cleaning for 1BHK to 3BHK flats: floors, bathrooms, kitchen, windows
and balcony. Cleaning material and machines are brought by our team.
Takes about 4 hours for a 2BHK.
